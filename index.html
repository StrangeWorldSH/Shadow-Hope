About Strange World

Who Are We?

We are a creative studio focused on the intersection of psychological horror and digital technology. Through the Shadow Hope Saga, we transform complex ideas into interactive experiences, leveraging the Godot engine and 3D modeling to build worlds that challenge the player's perception.

Our Mission

To develop innovative horror games that go beyond jump scares, immersing users in deep narratives and haunting atmospheres. We optimize every line of code and every polygon to deliver high-quality technical and emotional impact.

Our Vision

To become a benchmark in the independent game development scene, leading the creation of new cult sagas in the horror genre. We aim to set a standard of technical excellence and expand our creative ecosystem into viral marketing and high-end audiovisual production.
